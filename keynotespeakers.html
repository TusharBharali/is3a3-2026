Keynote Speaker

Dr. Ashutosh Dutta – IEEE Fellow

Chief 5G Strategist at The Johns Hopkins University, USA.

Dr. Ashutosh Dutta is an internationally recognized researcher and technology strategist in the areas of wireless communications, 5G/6G networks, edge computing, cybersecurity, and AI-driven intelligent networking systems. He currently serves as Chief 5G Strategist at The Johns Hopkins University, USA. An IEEE Fellow, Dr. Dutta has made significant contributions to next-generation communication technologies through his leadership roles in academia, industry, and international standardization activities. His research focuses on intelligent and secure future communication infrastructures, mobile edge computing, and resilient cyber-physical systems.

Keynote Speaker

Dr. M Sohel Rahman

Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)

Dr. M Sohel Rahman is a distinguished academic and researcher at the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). His research interests span bioinformatics, graph algorithms, pattern matching, computational biology, and healthcare informatics. He has contributed extensively to interdisciplinary computational research with numerous publications in reputed international journals and conferences. His work bridges algorithmic innovations with real-world biological and healthcare applications, making impactful contributions to data-driven life science research.

Keynote Speaker

Dr. Francesco Flammini

Mälardalen University, Sweden

Dr. Francesco Flammini is a renowned researcher and academic currently associated with Mälardalen University, Sweden. His research expertise includes cybersecurity, dependable systems, industrial informatics, critical infrastructure protection, AI-driven safety systems, and smart transportation technologies. He has authored several influential research publications and actively collaborates with academia and industry on resilient and intelligent cyber-physical systems. His work emphasizes trustworthy AI, digital transformation, and secure automation for next-generation smart infrastructures.
