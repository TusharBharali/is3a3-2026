Welcome to IS³A³ 2026

About the Symposium

The International Symposium on Smart Systems, Algorithms and Applications (IS³A³) 2026 is a premier global forum for researchers, engineers, and practitioners to share their latest findings and innovations in the rapidly evolving landscape of intelligent technologies.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between theoretical algorithmic research and practical smart system implementations. This year, we focus on resilient architectures, autonomous systems, and the ethical implications of AI-driven technologies.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there presentation awards?
Yes, we have "Best Paper" and "Best Student Presentation" awards which include a certificate and a cash prize.
Will the proceedings be indexed?
Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore and major indexing databases (Scopus, WoS).
Is accommodation provided?
While we don't provide free accommodation, we have tie-ups with nearby hotels and the University Guest House for discounted rates.
Can I attend without presenting?
Absolutely! You can register as a "Non-presenting Delegate" to attend all sessions and network with experts.
