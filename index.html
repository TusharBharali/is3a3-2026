Symposium Framework

Strategic Vision & Core Aims

Empowering Tomorrow's Intelligent Ecosystems

The International Symposium on Smart Systems, Algorithms and Applications (IS³A³ 2026) aims to provide a premier global platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to present and discuss recent advances in Smart Systems, Artificial Intelligence, Network & Cyber Security, Algorithms, and Emerging Applications.

IS³A³ 2026 Secure & Intelligent

Knowledge Dissemination

Promote dissemination of quality research and emerging technological trends to advance the global landscape of technical engineering literature.

Industrial & Academic Synapses

Encourage strong collaboration and knowledge-sharing infrastructures between academia, cutting-edge industries, and global research organizations.

Intelligent Exchange Pipelines

Facilitate the robust exchange of foundational and novel ideas targeting the optimization of secure, intelligent, and scalable computing systems.

Scholarly Interactivity

Provide premium opportunities for continuous mentorship and professional interaction among veteran researchers, active professionals, and young scholars.

Cross-Boundary Engineering

Support interdisciplinary research initiatives specifically engineered to address complex, real-world structural challenges and societal applications.

Explore the Region

Places to Visit in Tezpur

Discover the scenic beauty and rich heritage of Tezpur (The Cultural Capital of Assam and The City of Eternal Romance) and its surroundings.

~11 km

Agnigarh Hill

A mythological hillock associated with Princess Usha and Prince Aniruddha. Offers panoramic views of the Brahmaputra.

~11 km

Mahabhairab Temple

One of the oldest Shiva temples in Assam, dedicated to Lord Shiva in his fierce Bhairava form. It was patronized by Ahom rulers.

~15 km

Da Parbatia

Ancient archaeological site with a beautifully carved stone doorframe from the 5th-6th century Gupta period.

~12 km

Chitralekha Udyan (Cole Park)

A scenic park adorned with historic stone sculptures, pathways, and dynamic water features ideal for evening relaxation.

~11 km

Bamuni Hills

Famous for artistic sculptural ruins from the 9th-10th century, displaying exceptional historical stone carvings.

~33 km

Kaziranga National Park

A world heritage site widely celebrated as the natural home to the world's largest population of great Indian one-horned rhinoceros.

~13 km

Tezpur Airport (Salonibari)

Domestic airport with connectivity to major cities. Located at Salonibari, serving Tezpur and surrounding areas.

~12 km

Dekargaon Railway Station

Nearest railway station to Tezpur University, well-connected to Guwahati and other major cities of India.

~32 km

Nameri National Park

Biodiversity hotspot along the Jia Bhoroli River, perfect for river rafting and bird watching.

Distances are approximate from Tezpur University campus

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there presentation awards?
Yes, we have "Best Paper" and "Best Student Presentation" awards which include a certificate and a cash prize.
Will the proceedings be indexed?
Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore and major indexing databases (Scopus, WoS).
Is accommodation provided?
While we don't provide free accommodation, we have tie-ups with nearby hotels and the University Guest House for discounted rates.
Can I attend without presenting?
Absolutely! You can register as a "Non-presenting Delegate" to attend all sessions and network with experts.
