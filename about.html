About the Symposium

The International Symposium on Smart Systems, Algorithms and Applications (IS³A³) 2026 is a premier global forum for researchers, engineers, and practitioners to share their latest findings and innovations in the rapidly evolving landscape of intelligent computing technologies.

Our core mission is to bridge the gap between profound theoretical algorithmic research and practical, deployable smart system implementations. This year, the symposium is heavily focused on resilient architectures, autonomous decision-making systems, and the ethical implications of AI-driven technologies.
