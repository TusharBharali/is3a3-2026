Doctoral Colloquium

A vibrant forum for PhD scholars to present, discuss, and refine their research tracks under expert mentorship.

About the Doctoral Colloquium

IS³A³ 2026 is delighted to host a Doctoral Colloquium, an exclusive forum for PhD scholars to showcase their innovation. The primary objective of the Doctoral Colloquium is to provide a platform for in-depth research discussions, constructive feedback, and professional mentoring while fostering interaction among emerging researchers and experts from academia and industry. The colloquium offers doctoral students a unique opportunity to present and discuss their ongoing research contributions across the broad thematic areas of IS³A³ 2026, including smart systems, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, algorithms, optimization, and emerging interdisciplinary applications.

Submission Guidelines

Eligibility

Senior doctoral students who have completed at least two years of their Ph.D. research work and have not yet submitted their thesis are eligible to participate in the Doctoral Colloquium.

Extended Abstract Format

Eligible participants are invited to submit a 2-page extended abstract highlighting their ongoing research contributions and outcomes achieved so far. The abstract must be prepared on standard A4 size paper with 1-inch top and bottom margins and 1.25-inch left and right margins. The document should use Times New Roman font with a minimum font size of 11 pt. Relevant figures, tables, and illustrations may be included wherever appropriate.

The extended abstract should include the following components:

  • Title, name, and affiliation of the candidate
  • Name and affiliation of the supervisor(s)
  • Background and motivation
  • Problem statement
  • Proposed methodology/approach
  • Results or significant findings, if available
  • Discussion and future research directions

Participants are also required to submit a valid student identity card in PDF/JPG/PNG format, along with a certificate from the Head of the Department certifying that the candidate is in the final stage of Ph.D. research and is willing to participate in the IS³A³ 2026 Doctoral Colloquium.

Review Process

All submitted abstracts will be evaluated by an expert review committee. Based on reviewers’ scores, recommendations, and overall assessment, selected submissions will be invited for poster presentation during the Doctoral Colloquium.

Awards

The Doctoral Colloquium will feature awards for outstanding poster presentations. The awards will recognize originality, research quality, innovation, and presentation effectiveness. Evaluation will be carried out by an expert panel considering both the technical merit of the submitted abstract and the clarity and impact of the presentation.

Important Dates

Submission Deadline: October 15, 2026
Notification of Acceptance: October 31, 2026

Registration

At least one author registration is mandatory for every accepted Doctoral Colloquium presentation. However, if the candidate has already registered under a regular symposium paper, no separate registration is required. Poster presentations will be scheduled only if the primary doctoral student author is registered for the symposium.
