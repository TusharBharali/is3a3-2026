Call For Papers

Join the international research community at IS³A³ 2026 to present original and innovate work.

Submissions

Original high-quality research papers are invited from researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and members of the international research community for presentation at IS³A³ 2026. All submissions will undergo a rigorous double-blind peer-review process based on originality, technical quality, relevance, and significance.

Submit Your Paper Download CFP Brochure
Authors may submit:
  • Long Papers:  up to 8 pages
  • Short Papers:  up to 4 pages

Submissions should present original and unpublished work and must not be under consideration elsewhere.

Publication

Selected peer-reviewed papers will be considered for publication in series Advances in Computer Science Applications and Research (ACSAR) by the internationally recognized publishing house Springer.

Special Issue Journal

A few high-quality extended papers may also be considered for publication in a Special Issue of a reputed journal
(under discussion and subject to confirmation).

Formatting & Process Notes

  • Submissions are expected to present original, unpublished research contributions and should not be under consideration elsewhere.
  • Manuscripts must strictly conform to the Springer LNCS template formatting specifications.
  • Regular research tracks are restricted to a maximum sequence length allocation of 8 pages.
  • All submission pipeline phases are managed transparently via the Springer Nature Meteor Submission System.
